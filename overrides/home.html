New in 3.0Burp Suite connector

The integration you did not expect.
Burp Suite, inside reNgine.

You can send a finding to Burp Repeater in one click. The request opens in Burp exactly as nuclei sent it, so you can test it by hand straight away. The requests you then make in Burp return to reNgine as endpoints, which keeps your manual testing and your scan results in one place.
